What V7 Changes

ATR now uses real High/Low/Close candles. Regimes use EMA50 vs EMA200 plus EMA200 slope: bull when trend is clearly rising, bear when clearly falling, otherwise sideways. This reduces the mistake of forcing one strategy to trade in every type of market.

V10 Logic

Bull regime = price above EMA200, EMA50 above EMA200, EMA200 rising, and ADX above a minimum threshold. Breakout entries require a Donchian high breakout. Pullback entries require a dip toward EMA20/EMA50 followed by a rebound. Exits use an ATR initial stop plus ATR trailing stop, or regime failure.

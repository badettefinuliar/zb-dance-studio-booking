How to Read This

A strategy can look strong in training and fail out-of-sample. We are looking for positive or near-flat out-of-sample return, controlled drawdown, profit factor above 1.0, and ideally 10+ out-of-sample trades. Compare each strategy against the OOS Buy & Hold benchmark too.

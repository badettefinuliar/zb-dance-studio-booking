V8 Logic

Entry requires a sideways regime, price at or below the lower Bollinger Band, oversold RSI, acceptable ATR volatility, and non-spike volume. Exit uses Bollinger mean recovery, RSI recovery, or fixed stop/take levels. The lab evaluates the same setups across many separate 30-day out-of-sample windows.
